# Minimal guide entry — the smallest outline that still teaches

Use this skeleton when you write your own make.fun guide (for example for a
future guide bounty). It is an outline, not a finished entry: every heading
below needs your own words, your own referral code, and at least one revision
pass before it can pass the transcript gate.

1. **Title and badges** — your guide's title, then the bounty's three live
   make.fun badge lines with your own owner and repository filled in.
2. **Referral block, early and honest** — your link and bare code in the first
   screenful, with one sentence on what a referral is and that it costs the
   reader nothing.
3. **Quickstart** — the whole platform in five to seven numbered steps.
4. **What the reader needs** — account, GitHub, Node.js 22, a Solana wallet.
5. **The walkthrough** — sign up, link accounts, pick a bounty, get the kit,
   build, bundle, check, submit, claim. One short section per step, with the
   exact command for each.
6. **How referrals pay you** — first-touch binding, the 60-day window, review
   before approval, and claiming with your other rewards.
7. **Troubleshooting** — at least the quota (HTTP 429), badge, About, and
   authorship failures, each with its fix.
8. **Questions** — cost, coding skill, legitimacy, agents, transcripts.
9. **Footer** — your referral link and code once more, labelled as yours.

Rules that make or break this outline in practice:

- The file must be named exactly `README.md` and sit at the repository root.
- A screenshot of the real, published repository page goes beside it as
  `screenshot.png`.
- Write the guide inside the recorded agent session, then revise it in a
  later message of the same session. Pasting a finished guide in fails the
  authorship check no matter how good the prose is.
